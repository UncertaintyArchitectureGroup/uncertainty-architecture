# Golden Scenarios and Reference Cases

Golden scenarios are curated cases used for regression, comparison, boundary review, and detection of unexpected change.

They may represent:

- expected ordinary behavior;
- critical invariants;
- acceptable variation;
- edge and adversarial cases;
- known failure modes;
- situations requiring escalation or human authority.

Golden scenarios are not universal ground truth. They need not contain one ideal output, and UA does not prescribe a fixed number of cases. Their value depends on risk coverage, representativeness, maintenance, and the decisions they support.
