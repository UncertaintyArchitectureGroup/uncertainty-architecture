# NBER WP 35275

Mert Demirer, Leon Musolff and Liyuan Yang. *Writing Code vs. Shipping Code: Productivity Effects Across Generations of AI Coding Tools*. May 2026. [Paper](https://www.nber.org/papers/w35275) · [PDF](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w35275/w35275.pdf).

Slide 4. Working paper, not peer reviewed. Observational matched event study, over 100,000 GitHub developers plus AI telemetry; not an RCT.

## Table 5, printed page 33, weeks 21–30

| Component | Changed LOC | Commits | Releases |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Autocomplete | +228.2% | +35.9% | +10.2% |
| Synchronous agents | +741.3% | +109.1% | +20.3% |
| Asynchronous agents | +658.3% | +33.6% | Not separately estimated |

Figure 1 (p. 3) combines components as cumulative levels: **17.3× LOC, 2.8× commits, 1.3× releases**. Example: 1 + (228.2 + 741.3 + 658.3)/100 = 17.278. Do not multiply these estimates or call them a conversion rate. Changed LOC includes additions and deletions.

Figure 12 (p. 41), §8.2: new-cohort usage proxies during the first three months show no corresponding aggregate increase across the studied marketplace panels. This is not a welfare measure or the whole installed-software market. The share below 10 ratings/downloads rises from 79% to 86% for iOS and from 18% to 31% for Chrome.
