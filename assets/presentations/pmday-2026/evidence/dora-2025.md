# DORA 2025: perception, throughput and stability

Used on slide 4. [Official summary](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/announcing-the-2025-dora-report), [report entry](https://dora.dev/research/2025/dora-report/), [errata](https://dora.dev/research/2025/errata/). Checked 2026-09-21.

## Numbers directly visible in the downloaded infographic

Source: Google LLC / DORA, [official infographic PDF](originals/dora-2025-infographic.pdf), page 1. Visual transcription, not an estimate from chart geometry.

| Figure | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 90% | Respondents use AI at work |
| >80% | Respondents perceive an improvement in their productivity |
| 59% | Respondents perceive a positive effect on code quality |
| Nearly 5,000 | Survey respondents; the research also includes over 100 hours of qualitative material |

These are **shares of respondents**, not percentages of speed or quality improvement. The infographic is image-only; these rows provide searchable text.

## Separate delivery outcome

The official authors' summary describes positive associations between AI adoption and delivery throughput/product performance, and a negative association with delivery stability. **Delivery instability and code quality are different outcomes.** The survey can therefore report perceived code-quality gains alongside worse delivery stability.

The inspected public material provides no verified numeric stability coefficient here. Do not invent a percentage decrease or import a 2024 coefficient. The full report download currently leads to a contact form and was not obtained. The errata identifies report version 2025.2; that does not mean its full text was reviewed.

Slide wording: >80% report productivity improvement; 59% report code-quality improvement; adoption is also associated with lower delivery stability. Associations and perceptions do not establish causal effects.
