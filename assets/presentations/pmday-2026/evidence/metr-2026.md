# METR AI usage survey, 11 May 2026

Slide 4. [Primary publication](https://metr.org/blog/2026-05-11-ai-usage-survey/), *Methodology* and *Results / Productivity gains around March 2026*. Checked 2026-09-21.

- 349 retained technical-worker respondents from a convenience sample.
- Median self-reported work-value multipliers range from **1.4× to 2× across question formulations**.
- Median self-reported task-speed multiplier is **3×**.

The range is not a confidence interval. Perceived speed and perceived valuable output are distinct, and neither directly measures causal delivery uplift. Selection and elicitation biases matter.

Historical context: [METR's July 2025 RCT](https://metr.org/blog/2025-07-10-early-2025-ai-experienced-os-dev-study/) found 19% longer completion time for its experienced open-source developers using early-2025 tools (16 developers, 246 tasks). Do not project that result onto every current agent. The [February 2026 update](https://metr.org/blog/2026-02-24-uplift-update/) identifies selection problems in the updated experiment; no universal contemporary percentage is inferred here.

This is a local summary, not a redistributed full article or a replication.
