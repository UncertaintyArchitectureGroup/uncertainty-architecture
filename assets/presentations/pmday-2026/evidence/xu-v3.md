# Xu et al., arXiv:2510.10165v3

Feiyang (Amber) Xu, Poonacha K. Medappa, Murat M. Tunc, Martijn Vroegindeweij and Jan C. Fransoo. *AI-Assisted Programming Decreases the Productivity of Experienced Developers by Increasing the Technical Debt and Maintenance Burden*. [Version 3, 28 January 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.10165v3). Checked 2026-09-21.

Slide 4 notes. Sections 4 and 6: the upper prior-activity quartile has **19% fewer commits and 6.5% more reviews**; the lower quartile has **43.5% more commits and 17.7% more PRs**. Project-level PR rework increases **2.4%**.

The study concerns **2020–2022 data and early Copilot**. The 2026 version date does not make the sample a study of 2026 agents. Activity quartiles do not establish employment grade; review counts do not measure review hours. These outcomes support a workload-redistribution discussion, not a universal decline in experienced engineers' productivity.
