# Formulation Provenance — Thinking Systems and the Arkadiy Dobkin Exchange

> **Record boundary:** This note preserves maintainer-attested dialogue provenance for the formulation **Thinking Systems**. It does not publish private correspondence verbatim and does not treat the exchange as independent evidence for the later UA engineering definition.

## Provenance claim

Vitalii Oborskyi reports that the formulation **“Thinking Systems”** entered the current research line through an exchange with **Arkadiy Dobkin** following discussion prompted by Dobkin's public *From Fall to Rise* LinkedIn post.

The public post provides context for the surrounding discussion. This repository does **not** claim that the phrase itself can be independently verified from the public post, and the provenance record therefore remains explicitly maintainer-attested.

## Attribution boundary

The attribution is intentionally narrow:

- **credited to Dobkin:** the formulation that helped name and sharpen the research boundary;
- **not attributed to Dobkin:** authorship of the UA-specific Thinking-System definition, Consequential Runtime Responsibility, the controlled-object argument, the capability model, four-horizon lifecycle model, or the resulting paper;
- **not implied:** endorsement, agreement, co-authorship, framework authority, or validation of UA.

## Current research status

The phrase is now used by UA as the current system-category term, with the canonical definition owned by the draft-normative glossary. Provenance of the formulation remains separate from authority over that definition.

**Register item:** `TS-TERM-001`.

## Why this record exists

Dialogue provenance should not live only in acknowledgments or mutable article prose. When an external exchange materially introduces a term, framing, challenge, counterexample, or research question, the Research Track should preserve:

1. what entered the research;
2. through which source or exchange;
3. what evidentiary boundary applies;
4. what later meaning was developed independently;
5. whether the item remains research, became canonical, or was superseded.

This record is therefore both provenance for the current term and an example of the standard external-dialogue intake path defined by the Research Review Process.
