## Why the source matters

The article examines deployment-topology decisions across on-device, cloud, and hybrid LLM execution. It contributes research context for:

- data and privacy boundaries;
- offline and degraded operating modes;
- fallback and routing decisions;
- model and prompt lifecycle management across device and cloud releases;
- latency, quality, cost, and operational trade-offs;
- the relationship between deployment topology and control allocation.

## Use in synthesis
